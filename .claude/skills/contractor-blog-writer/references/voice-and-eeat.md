# Voice, E-E-A-T & Anti-Slop

## Who's writing

Homescape Construction is **owner-operated** — a master carpenter with 20+ years and a small
crew, serving Chicago and suburbs. The voice is that of a working contractor who's seen the
job go wrong a hundred ways and tells you the truth: practical, plain-spoken, specific,
quietly confident. Not a marketing department. Not a thought-leader.

Write like Dave explaining it to a neighbor over the fence — not like a brochure.

## Tone targets

- **Plain and direct.** Short words. Concrete nouns. Active voice.
- **Specific over impressive.** "Footings below the 42-inch frost line" not "expert foundation solutions."
- **Honest about tradeoffs.** Mention what's hard, what costs more, when to wait. Trust is built
  by admitting the unglamorous parts.
- **Local.** Name neighborhoods (Lincoln Park, Logan Square, Wicker Park, Avondale...), Chicago
  permit realities, weather and masonry seasonality. This is what makes posts un-clonable.
- **Varied rhythm.** Mix short and long sentences. Don't let every paragraph be the same shape.

## Honesty / E-E-A-T guardrails (hard rules)

- **Never invent** permit numbers, addresses, prices, client names/quotes, stats, certifications,
  award claims, or review counts. The audit found a schema claim of "200 reviews / 4.9" — do not
  echo or inflate numbers you can't stand behind.
- If a specific figure isn't supplied, use a **labeled range**: "typically $25,000–$40,000 in the
  Chicago market, depending on finishes." Never present a made-up point estimate as fact.
- Reconcile, don't repeat, the site's conflicting history claims (the audit found "since 1995" vs
  "founded 2012" vs "30+ years" vs "18 years"). Pick the true one with the user and use it
  consistently. Don't propagate the contradiction into new posts.
- Attribute to a **real person** when possible. AI engines and Google reward named authorship with
  demonstrable experience over "by the team."
- Client quotes require permission and should read like a real person, not a testimonial template.

## Banned phrases / AI-slop tells (do not use)

- "In today's market" / "In today's fast-paced world"
- "When it comes to [X]"
- "Look no further"
- "Nestled in the heart of"
- "Whether you're a... or a..., we've got you covered"
- "Elevate your space" / "transform your dreams into reality" / "bring your vision to life"
- "Top-notch," "state-of-the-art," "second to none," "unparalleled"
- Empty intro paragraphs that restate the title before saying anything
- Three CTAs stacked at the end (use one)
- Em-dash-and-rule-of-three patterns repeated in every paragraph

## Formatting that helps AEO/GEO

- **Lead answer first**: the 40–60 word paragraph right under the H1 is what gets pulled into
  AI answers and snippets. Make it self-contained.
- **Question H2s**: at least one heading in natural-question form.
- **Lists and one table**: scannable, and tables can win table snippets.
- **FAQ block**: 3–5 real questions, 40–60 word answers, mirrored into front-matter `faq`
  for FAQPage schema. This is the single highest-leverage AEO addition for this site.
