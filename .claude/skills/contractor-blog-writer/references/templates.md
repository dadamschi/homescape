# Post Templates

Pick the one matching the brief. Each is a skeleton — fill with real, specific detail.
All target the structure in SKILL.md (lead answer → question-phrased H2s → specifics → FAQ →
one soft CTA). Word floors: service/guide 700–1,200; project/seasonal 600–1,000;
permit-data 500–800.

---

## 1. Service (evergreen) — highest priority type

These are the pages that actually earn rankings, links, and AI citations. The audit found
the site had *none* of these as real pages.

```
# [Service] in Chicago: Costs, Timeline, and What to Expect

[40–60 word lead: what it costs / takes / involves, stated plainly with a Chicago range.]

## How much does [service] cost in Chicago?
- Cost range + what drives it (size, finishes, structural work, permits).
- A small table or bullet list of cost tiers.

## How long does it take?
- Realistic timeline incl. permit lead time.

## Do you need a permit in Chicago? [question-phrased H2]
- Honest, specific answer for the city + common suburbs.

## What [service] looks like with Homescape
- Your process; tie to a real project if possible (link it).

## Frequently asked questions
- 3–5 Q&As, 40–60 words each.

[One soft CTA → /contact]
```

## 2. Project (case study) — un-duplicatable by design

Built around a real job. The specifics are the moat.

```
# [Neighborhood] [Project Type]: [What Made It Interesting]

[40–60 word lead: the home, the goal, the outcome.]

## The challenge
- The real constraint (old wiring, load-bearing wall, tight lot, landmark district).

## What we did
- The approach, materials, key decisions. Specifics, not adjectives.

## The result
- Outcome + (with permission) a real client quote.

## What this means for your project
- Generalize one lesson a reader can use.

## FAQ  (2–4 Q&As)

[Soft CTA]
```
> Needs a **unique** `mainImage` (real project photo). Never the shared hero.

## 3. Permit-data (timely hook) — dedupe HARD

This is the cluster that got over-produced. **One canonical post per real angle, max.**
Only write one if there's a genuine, specific, verifiable permit record and a useful lesson.

```
# What a [$X] [Project] Permit Tells You About [Real Topic]

[40–60 word lead: the concrete number + why it's useful to a homeowner.]

## The numbers
- Cost, sq ft, $/sq ft, city fees. Cite the public record type/source (don't invent it).

## What drives the cost
- 3–5 real factors (bulleted).

## How your project might compare
- Ranges for smaller/larger scope.

## FAQ (2–3 Q&As)

[Soft CTA]
```
> **Anti-duplication rule:** if a post already covers "express permit processing times" or
> "solar + battery standard," do NOT write another. Update the existing one with the new
> data point instead. See `rewrite-playbook.md`.

### Shared rules for every template
- Exactly one `| Homescape Construction` in `seo.metaTitle`; none appended inside `title`.
- At least one H2 phrased as a natural question.
- At least one concrete number, list, OR table.
- FAQ answers 40–60 words; mirror them into the `faq` front-matter for FAQPage schema.
- Leave `publishedAt` blank (drip-publish plan assigns it).
