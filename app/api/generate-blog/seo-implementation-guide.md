# On-Page SEO Implementation Guide

## Meta Tags

Every page needs a unique meta title (under 60 characters) and meta description (under 155 characters) that includes the primary keyword for that page.

**Example for Custom Builds page:**

- **Meta Title:** Custom Home Builder Chicago | New Construction | Homescape
- **Meta Description:** Chicago custom home builder with 20+ years experience. Get a free estimate for new construction, tear-down rebuilds & design-build projects. Call (773) 934-0589.

## Header Tag Structure

Each page should have exactly one H1 containing the primary keyword. Use H2s for major sections and H3s for subsections. Sprinkle secondary keywords into H2s and H3s naturally.

## Internal Linking Strategy

Cross-link between service pages and location pages. For example, the Remodeling page should link to each location page where remodeling is mentioned, and each location page should link back to the relevant service page. This distributes page authority and helps Google understand your site structure.

## Schema Markup Recommendations

Add LocalBusiness schema to every page with your business name, address, phone number, service area, and aggregate rating. Add Service schema to each service page. Add FAQ schema to any page with a Q&A section to earn rich snippets in search results.

## Google Business Profile Optimization

Ensure your Google Business Profile lists all services, service areas (Chicago + every suburb you serve), and uses the same NAP (Name, Address, Phone) as your website. Post project photos regularly and respond to every review to boost local rankings.

## Content Velocity Recommendation

Publish at least 2 blog posts per month targeting long-tail variations of your core keywords. Examples:

- "How Much Does a Kitchen Remodel Cost in Lincoln Park?"
- "Chicago ADU Zoning Rules: What Homeowners Need to Know in 2026"
- "Basement Finishing in Oak Park: Timeline, Cost, and What to Expect"
- "Tear-Down vs. Renovation: Which Makes Sense for Your Chicago Home?"

Each post should link back to the relevant service page.
